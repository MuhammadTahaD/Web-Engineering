Breakfast

Menemen

Ingredients: Eggs, tomatoes, green peppers, olive oil, salt and black pepper.

Instructions: Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the peppers. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft. Add the eggs and gently mix everything together. Cook until the eggs are ready and serve hot with bread.

Turkish Scrambled Eggs with Tomatoes
Lunch

Doner Kebab

Ingredients: Chicken or beef, yogurt, garlic, paprika, cumin, black pepper, salt, olive oil and flatbread.

Instructions: Marinate the meat with yogurt, garlic and spices. Cook the meat until well done and slice it into thin pieces. Serve the meat inside flatbread with vegetables and yogurt sauce.

Turkish Doner Kebab
Dinner

İmam Bayıldı

Ingredients: Eggplant, onions, tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, parsley, salt and black pepper.

Instructions: Cut the eggplants and cook them lightly in olive oil. Prepare a filling with onions, tomatoes and garlic. Stuff the eggplants with the filling and bake until the eggplants become soft.

Stuffed Turkish Eggplant
Dessert

Baklava

Ingredients: Phyllo pastry, pistachios, butter, sugar, water and lemon juice.

Instructions: Layer the phyllo pastry with melted butter and chopped pistachios. Bake until golden and crispy. Prepare a sugar syrup with water and lemon juice and pour it over the hot baklava. Allow it to cool before serving.

Turkish Baklava
